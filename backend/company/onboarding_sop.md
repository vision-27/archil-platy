# Northstar client onboarding SOP � synthetic
Owner: Delivery Operations. Applies to all closed_won HubSpot deals with a known nonnegative value_usd; there is no minimum contract value.
Required documents: signed agreement and brand assets. Pause and report missing documents before creating anything.
For an eligible deal with both documents, create one client workspace and a delivery checklist.
Schedule a kickoff with the account owner and delivery lead after workspace creation.
Update the client tracker with workspace, checklist and kickoff status. Repeated requests must not create duplicates.
Ineligible deals are skipped with a reason. Missing documents yield needs_documents and a list of missing items.
Successful onboarding yields completed with action records and the client identifier.
